How good is the model?

We trained on 1998 to 2016 and then looked once at 2017 to 2019. A "miss" is how far a guess was from the real yield, as a share of the real yield. We use the middle miss, so a few wild years do not decide the score.

When last year's yield is known, simply repeating it is as good as our model. The model is useful when there is no history to copy.

What to keep in mind

  • The model sees the district, crop, season, year and the usual rain of the district. It does not see that year's weather, water, soil or seed, so it cannot tell a bad year from a good one.
  • For that reason we do not offer a guess for next year. Without weather for that year, it could only give an ordinary value.
  • Our records end in 2019, and only three of those years were used as the final test. A score from three years can move a lot.
  • Yield is in tonnes per hectare. Some very small crops have only a few records in a district.
