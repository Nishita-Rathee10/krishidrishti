About

Krishi Drishti is a college project made by Nishita at Lovely Professional University, for a course on supervised learning.

It looks at farm data for the districts of Punjab and Haryana: how much rain and heat each district gets, how much a crop yields, and which crops the climate of a district may suit.

What this is not

It is not advice for farmers. The models see rain, temperature and humidity, and nothing about a field's water, soil, seed or market. Please do not change what you grow because of this site.

It does not promise rain, yield or prices. Where our models were not better than a simple rule, the pages say so.

Why Punjab and Haryana

The two states have good district records of crops and rain, and they grow a mix of crops that makes the comparison interesting. We kept to two states so that every number could be checked.

Read how we built it to see the data and the tests.

Built with

Python, Flask, scikit-learn, statsmodels, SHAP and LIME. The site runs on Render, and the code is on GitHub.
