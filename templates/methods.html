{{ _("What we left out, and why") }}

  • {{ _("Fertilizer advice. A fair tool needs soil test results for each farm or block. We do not have them in a usable form, and a guess from climate alone would mislead.") }}
  • {{ _("Market prices. The price files we found were either very messy or too short to test a forecast fairly, so we do not forecast prices.") }}
  • {{ _("Water, soil and seed. Canals, tube wells, soil type and seed variety shape a farm's yield. None of them is in our data.") }}
  • {{ _("The future. Our records end in 2015 for rain and 2019 for crops. The yield model does not see a year's weather, so it cannot say anything useful about a year to come.") }}
